02 The CraftViz platform

One platform, three breakthroughs — built for the way buyers actually decide.

We didn't digitize the spreadsheet. We replaced it with a system designed around how senior buyers explore, visualize, and commit to choices about the home they'll spend the next decade in.

01 / Catalog

Studio-quality 3D for every plan.

Each floor plan in your catalog ships with a precision-modeled 3D environment — crafted in Blender, photorealistic, ready to serve thousands of buyer sessions without a single render delay.

  • Hand-modeled GLB assets, accurate to scale
  • Photorealistic lighting and materials
  • One model, every option combination
02 / Visualize

Walk through it in 3D.

Parsed plans become real-time, navigable 3D rooms. Buyers see exactly how their flooring, walls, cabinets, and fixtures look together — orbit, zoom, switch options live.

  • Three.js rendering, no plugins required
  • Material swaps update instantly
  • Works on tablet, desktop, and showroom kiosks
03 / Sell

Lock in selections, error-free.

Every choice is priced, validated, and logged — then exported as a signed PDF or pushed straight to your construction system via webhook. No more reconciliation.

  • Vendor-validated option catalogs
  • Auto-priced upgrade summary
  • Direct API to ERP / construction tools
